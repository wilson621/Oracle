## Architecture and lifecycle

R4 retains the corrected launcher/controller directory ownership boundary and the
race-tolerant, ownership-verified process teardown policy. A disappeared PID is
accepted only after prior ownership verification, a failed stop request and a fresh
absence check. Surviving, reused, ambiguous and unowned identities fail closed.

The exact accepted R6 public certificate is temporarily trusted, the hash-bound
MSIX is installed, attempt-scoped LocalState configuration is consumed, and only
the ownership-verified packaged process and loopback listener may serve the ten
unchanged Stage 4 journeys.

## Authority boundary

The preparation baseline records qualification execution, transfer preparation,
authority creation and attempt execution as false, with maximum attempts zero. A
separately committed execution-enabled overlay must bind this preparation commit
and tree and permit one create-only transfer and at most one governed attempt.

Authority remains prohibited until transfer integrity, independent verification,
fresh host continuity, elevation, approved-tool identity, zero state, create-only
roots, trust, security, network isolation and return-root gates all pass. Stage 5
and later work remain unauthorised.
