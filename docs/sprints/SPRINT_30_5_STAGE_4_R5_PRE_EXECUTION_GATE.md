execution overlay must establish and independently verify all of the following:

1. the exact accepted R8 MSIX, certificate, signer and R13 evidence bindings;
2. a fresh create-only transfer and fresh clean-host continuity;

6. no repository or prohibited development tool on `Founder-QA-01`;
7. an isolated private on-link route between exactly the two admitted hosts,
   with no active IPv4 or IPv6 default route on either host;
8. exactly two future loopback relay mappings for ports 54321 and 54324, with
   PostgreSQL and all other ports prohibited;
9. accepted R4 image digests and migrations already available without network
   retrieval;
10. fresh provider, account, session, mail, runtime, authority, attempt and
    evidence namespaces;
11. create-only secret handoff custody with all credential values excluded from
    evidence; and
12. a complete two-host teardown plan that is mandatory after every terminal
    outcome.

network, tool, image, zero-state and custody gate. A future authority may allow
at most one attempt and may never authorise a retry after consumption.
