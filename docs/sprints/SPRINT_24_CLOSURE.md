improvement rather than arbitrary engagement.

XP, Achievements, Missions and progression may be awarded only from verified
Evidence through Progression Service using governed deterministic exactly-once
accounting. AI output, renderer processes, browser components, clients and
other non-authoritative components cannot award or bypass progression.

Session Evidence. Coaching effectiveness remains correlational unless stronger
methodology is separately approved. Browser-owned progression mutation remains
permanently retired.
