The approved Founder Acceptance Criteria require the Founder to be able to
trace a claim from Session Evidence through suspected candidate, acceptance,
contradiction, dispute, supersession and deletion. Engineering has completed
and certified that trace. Formal Sprint acceptance and closure is a governance
decision; engineering cannot self-approve it.

- the recurring game-pattern accumulation boundary satisfies ADR-034–036;
- Operator Intelligence Service remains the sole claim-lifecycle authority;
- Memory remains a specialised producer;
- Snapshot and Context outputs remain bounded projections;
- manifest version `1.2.0` exactly matches Web and Electron runtime inventory;
- no Migration 014 is required;
- implementation and certification evidence is accepted.

- deployment;
- execution of Migrations 010–013 in production;
- Gate C;

- persisted producer or consumer activation;
- production accumulation-policy values;
- raw observation retention;
- sensitive, AI-generated, cross-game or Operator-wide inference;
- External Companion trust-boundary changes.

If accepted, Sprint 22 may close and engineering may assess Sprint 23 under the
approved Engineering Programme.
