# Founder Acceptance

The Founder accepted Sprint 27 as a complete engineering and architectural
Sprint on 25 July 2026. The accepted basis is:

- source implementation complete;
- source and synthetic certification passed;
- ADR-044 and ADR-045 boundaries preserved;
- manifest version `1.6.0` mechanically equal across Web and Electron; and
- the unavailable third-party runtime represented honestly rather than
  converted into false operational confidence.

Engineering completion, source certification and operational certification
are distinct governance milestones. Closing this Sprint does not promote the
Minecraft compatibility certificate or claim operational support.

# Deferred Operational Certification

Minecraft: Java Edition was not purchased or installed solely for testing.
Consequently, attached-window observation against the exact approved profile
has not been operationally tested.

The programme status remains:

> **Operational Certification Deferred — Required Test Environment
> Unavailable**

Under ADR-045, the exact compatibility profile remains
`provisionally-certified`. The uncertain observation capability remains
disabled and fail-closed. Oracle must make no operational-support claim for
Minecraft until an authorised tester successfully verifies the exact approved
profile.

Future live certification creates new operational evidence under ADR-045. It
does not reopen, rewrite or alter the accepted historical Sprint 27 record.

# Accepted Boundaries

Sprint 27 delivered the bounded Windows-only, exact-version, single-player,
local and ephemeral reference integration described in its Plan. It did not
prove live attached-window observation or broader Minecraft compatibility.

This closure grants no authority for:

- production deployment or activation;
- any database migration;
- runtime persistence or persisted producers or consumers;
- raw-frame, screenshot, clip, observation, Guidance or progress retention;
- upload or external processing;
- multiplayer, Realms, servers, mods, add-ons, plugins or game APIs;
- AI-generated Guidance or automated gameplay;
- authoritative Session, Evidence, Understanding, Mission or Progression
  mutation;
- Gate C, Guidance v2 or Desktop Platform API v2; or
- any External Companion trust-boundary change.
