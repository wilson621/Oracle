## Accepted delivery

The Founder accepted the Sprint 23 implementation and certification evidence.

generation. Reports are constructed from authoritative completed Sessions,
admitted Evidence and eligible governed Understanding through deterministic
engines and approved Game Integration boundaries.

No Migration 014 was required by Sprint 23.

## Permanent directive

Future report generation must preserve:

- Evidence admission through approved Game Integration boundaries;
- deterministic analysis as the primary factual authority;
- governed confidence and disagreement handling;
- replay protection and report history;
- renderer-safe projections; and
- graceful degradation when optional model enrichment is unavailable.

Language models may enrich presentation but must never become the authoritative
source of factual Session analysis. Prompt-only analysis authority is
permanently retired.
