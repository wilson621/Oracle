**Status:** Engineering preparation passed; authority not created
**Classification:** Pre-authority governance record
**Last Reviewed:** 4 August 2026

The Founder has authorised one complete R4 mission, but the committed preparation
baseline cannot create a transfer, authority or attempt. A separate execution
overlay must bind the reviewed preparation commit, tree and manifest, encode a new
single-use token, enable one create-only transfer and permit at most one attempt.

Before authority creation, the exact transferred payload must pass manifest,
custody and independent complete-inventory verification, host continuity,
Administrator admission, tool and image identity, reparse-free paths, fresh zero
state, create-only authority/evidence/return roots, security, trust and absence of
every active IPv4 or IPv6 default route. The harness verifies network isolation and
does not change network configuration.

A consumed authority or permanent failed attempt cannot be retried. No R1, R2 or
R3 identity, transfer, authority, attempt or evidence namespace may be reused.
