# SPRINT 28 PLAN — UNIFIED ORACLE PRODUCT EXPERIENCE

**Status:** Complete — Founder-accepted and closed
**Approved option:** Option A — Governed Full-Journey Convergence
**ADR required:** No
**Migration:** None
**Deployment:** Not authorised

---

# Objective

Turn Oracle's implemented capabilities into one coherent, truthful and
testable Beta product journey. Every retained surface must satisfy both
architectural truth and Operator value.

The journey must let an Operator understand:

- what Oracle knows;
- why Oracle believes it;
- what remains uncertain or inactive;
- what the Operator should do next;
- how progress will be understood over time;
- where trust, privacy, consent and limitations are visible; and
- why another verified Session will create value.

COD/Warzone remains Oracle's first proving ground. Minecraft remains a bounded
reference profile only; its certificate remains `provisionally-certified`,
observation remains disabled and operational certification remains deferred.

# Governing Boundaries

- Existing authoritative Services retain factual, lifecycle and mutation
  ownership.
- React, Web renderers and Electron renderers remain presentation-only.
- Retained pages consume only renderer-safe projections or explicit inactive,
  unavailable, degraded or deferred presentation state.
- No mock or fabricated state may stand in for inactive capability.
- ADR-040 manifest equality remains mandatory.
- ADR-041 through ADR-045 ownership, evidence, privacy, consent, retention and
  trust boundaries remain unchanged.
- Runtime persistence and persisted producers and consumers remain disabled.

# Delivery Phases

## Phase 1 — Product Truth Inventory

1. Inventory every route and navigation entry.
2. Trace its current data source and ownership.
3. Assess Architectural Truth and Operator Value independently.
4. Record `retain`, `consolidate`, `defer`, `redirect` or `remove`.
5. Establish the surviving Founder Beta journey before broad UI changes.

Exit evidence: [Sprint 28 Product Truth Inventory](SPRINT_28_PRODUCT_TRUTH_INVENTORY.md).

## Phase 2 — Canonical Product Shell

1. Establish one canonical shell and navigation source.
2. Remove the duplicate legacy Sidebar.
3. Provide desktop and responsive navigation with visible active state.
4. Add one shared vocabulary for active, inactive, unavailable, degraded and
   deferred capability.
5. Add accessible global not-found and error recovery.

## Phase 3 — Truthful Retained Surfaces

1. Build an Operator-facing Oracle home that explains present knowledge,
   uncertainty and next action.
2. Preserve the renderer-safe Companion experience and clarify platform,
   consent and certification limits.
3. Replace Session, Report, Understanding, Coaching and Progression legacy
   client calculations with truthful inactive-state projections until their
   persisted consumers are separately activated.
4. Consolidate duplicate Memory/DNA and Career/Achievement routes.
5. Add one Settings hub for permanent identity, security, privacy and support
   boundaries.
6. Remove production use of mock game and loadout data.

## Phase 4 — Web and Electron Certification

1. Verify all surviving routes and redirects.
2. Verify keyboard navigation, focus visibility, landmarks, status semantics
   and responsive layouts.
3. Verify Web absence of native authority.
4. Verify Electron receives only validated renderer-safe Companion state.
5. Verify the complete Founder Beta walkthrough.
6. Verify TypeScript, lint, production build, Electron compilation,
   dependency boundaries and manifest equality.

## Phase 5 — Closure Preparation

1. Produce implementation and certification evidence.
2. Reconcile the Roadmap, Engineering Programme, Master Build Plan, Project
   Board, Sprint Index and Implementation Status.
3. Prepare the Sprint 28 Founder Acceptance Package.
4. Stop for independent Founder review without deployment or push.

# Definition of Done

- Every route and navigation entry has an accepted inventory decision.
- One canonical product shell and navigation model remains.
- No retained surface depends on production mock data.
- No dead route, unsupported product claim or unexplained placeholder remains.
- Inactive capabilities say why they are inactive and what evidence or
  authority is required next.
- The Founder Beta walkthrough is coherent and useful even while persistence
  and operational Minecraft observation remain disabled.
- Web and Electron verification pass independently.
- Production, migrations, persistence, Gate C and trust boundaries remain
  unchanged.
